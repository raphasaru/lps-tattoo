ABOUT THE ARTIST

13 years in law enforcement.
A lifetime in tattooing.

Vinícius Grande is a Brazilian tattoo artist specializing in black and gray realism, large-scale projects and high-impact visual compositions.

That experience shaped his professional approach: discipline, responsibility, strategy and commitment to results.

His work is sought out by military personnel, law enforcement officers, family men and those who want to mark on their skin symbols of mission, faith, legacy, strength and identity.

During his last stay in Framingham, he tattooed clients from the Navy, Army and local law enforcement — building his name among those who value structure and professionalism.
