ABOUT

Structure before impact

Isa Piagetti is a Brazilian tattoo artist with 11 years of career, specializing in large-scale compositions, dark aesthetics, and high-impact visual constructions.

Her artistic foundation began before tattooing. Raised in a family of artists and with a background in Architecture, she developed structural vision, spatial reading, and a deep understanding of anatomy — fundamentals that define her work today.

With a consolidated trajectory in Brazil and Europe, she has been awarded at national and international conventions and served as a judge at European events. She has tattooed in Italy, Switzerland, and Germany, carrying her authorial signature to different audiences and cultures.

11 years of career

Her method is clear: structure before impact. Each project is born from study, plane organization, strategic contrast, and anatomical flow. Not images applied to skin. Visual constructions that follow movement, presence, and permanence.

THE PROCESS

How projects are built

Large-scale projects require method, planning, and respect for the skin. Large-scale works are her specialty: realism, surrealism, complex cover-ups, and dense compositions with dark aesthetic.

01

STRUCTURE

Every construction begins with body analysis: axis, movement, proportion, and visual hierarchy. Each element is positioned with intention, respecting anatomical flow and long-term readability.

02

LAYERING

Sessions are divided strategically to ensure depth, consistent contrast, and skin preservation. Building in layers is what sustains impact and durability over time.

03

SELECTION

Guest bookings go through curation. Selection prioritizes aesthetic alignment, project complexity, and commitment to the process. No improvisation. No generic solutions.

If you're looking for an original project built with artistic criteria, anatomical reading, and international standards — this is the moment to start the conversation.

Spots are limited and the schedule is organized in order of project approval.
