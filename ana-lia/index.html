ABOUT

Size doesn't define complexity

Ana Lia is a Brazilian tattoo artist with more than 6 years of experience, specializing in mini realism and delicate projects that unite refined technique with meaning.

Her work starts from a clear principle: size doesn't define complexity. In mini realism, every detail must be planned with precision — spacing, contrast, level of information, and long-term readability are decisions that determine not just the immediate result, but the permanence of the tattoo on the skin.

With a sensitive and technical artistic perspective, Ana develops projects that balance aesthetic lightness with symbolic depth — designed for women who seek expression, identity, and sophistication.

Her trajectory includes international sessions and participation as a judge at a tattoo convention in Europe, consolidating her work beyond Brazil.

THE PROCESS

How projects are built

Mini realism projects require precision, control, and deliberate decision-making at every detail. No improvisation. No generic solutions.

01

ADAPTATION

Every project begins with adapting the idea to the skin — considering scale, level of detail, contrast, and how the tattoo will behave over time. Each element must have function and clarity.

02

INTENTION

Scale reading, contrast control, strategic simplification, and definition of focal points. Ana works with authorial projects, tributes, symbolic elements, and delicate compositions — always tailored to the individual.

03

SELECTION

Guest bookings go through curation. Selection prioritizes alignment with the artist's aesthetic, clarity of the idea, and commitment to the process. Limited spots, scheduled in order of project approval.

If you're looking for a mini realism project built with care, precision, and intention — this is the moment to start the conversation.
