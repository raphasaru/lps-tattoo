RECOGNITION

25 Years, Over 40 Awards, and a Career That Crossed Borders

Built at conventions, international seasons, and by mentoring artists around the world

Kleber Soares' authority was built through consistency — 25 years dedicated to tattooing, creating projects that demand artistic vision, technical mastery, and deep responsibility for each client's story.

Over that journey, Kleber has won more than 40 awards at conventions, including Best of Show at the largest tattoo convention in Latin America — recognition given to the single best piece of the entire event.

That kind of award doesn't just validate a beautiful image. It validates technical decision-making, composition, contrast, application, finish, and visual impact.

Beyond client work, Kleber has become a reference point for other professionals, mentoring hundreds of artists around the world. Teaching requires mastery of the entire process, technical clarity, and experience that goes far beyond execution alone.

His international guest appearances reinforce this recognition: a Brazilian artist taking his technique abroad and being sought by clients who want to be tattooed by someone the market itself has validated.

Getting tattooed by Kleber means accessing a perspective built over decades — recognized by awards, clients, and the artists who learned from his technique.
