ABOUT

Your project starts with what you want to represent

Matheus Almeida is a Brazilian tattoo artist and founder of Apollo 33, developing authorial projects in Fine Line and Black & Grey Realism.

The process starts by understanding what you want to represent. Based on your story and references, Matheus develops an individual composition, designed to translate that meaning into a project that makes sense for you and for your body.

Every piece is built from scratch — no reused flash, no generic solutions. Just a project shaped around your story.

During the European season, Matheus will accept a limited number of clients in each destination, prioritizing projects with a clear reference and story.
