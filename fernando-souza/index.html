ABOUT

Who is Fernando Souza

Fernando Souza is a Brazilian tattoo artist and hyperrealism master with over 19 years of career, operating both in Brazil and internationally — developing authorial projects with a focus on construction, body reading, and lasting presence.

Over the course of his career, he accumulated more than 40 awards at tattoo conventions, establishing his name as a reference in high-level technical work. He has also been a mentor and educator for other tattoo artists for over 13 years in Brazil.

His work goes beyond aesthetics. Fernando eternizes histories on skin — transforming moments, memories, and connections into artistic compositions built with precision: light, shadow, depth, element composition, and body fit are all deliberate decisions so that the tattoo wears the body like a work of art.

THE PROCESS

More than technique — it's how your story is built on your skin

A hyperrealism tattoo demands more than technical ability. It demands reading, decision-making, and responsibility over what is being eternized. Sessions are organized respecting the skin's limits and the level of quality the delivery requires.

01

INITIAL INTERVIEW

Fernando conducts an initial conversation to understand which elements best represent the client's story. The goal is to translate identity — not just execute an image.

02

ART DEVELOPMENT

References are curated and the artwork is built: composition for ideal body fit, contrast planned for durability, and details adjusted to maintain readability over time. No improvisation.

03

SELECTION

The guest schedule is limited and organized in order of project approval. Priority goes to projects aligned with the artist's aesthetic, technical level, and artistic commitment to Realism.
