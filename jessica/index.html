Brazilian artist renowned for her glitter technique, bringing her signature to Paris and Amsterdam in a limited guest season.

Jessica's work doesn't start with a ready design. It starts with an idea that needs to be interpreted.

A work that has crossed borders

Throughout her career, Jessica has built a body of work that transcended national borders. She has brought her technique to studios across Europe — with sessions in countries like France and Portugal — and continues to expand that presence with new international seasons.

Today, she receives clients from different parts of the world, including Japan, Germany, and the UAE — people looking for more than a tattoo, but a project developed with intention and identity.

Beyond her sessions, she also works directly with the formation of other tattoo artists, through mentorships where she shares her technique, creative process, and development of artistic language.
