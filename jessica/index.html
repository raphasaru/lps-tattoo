GUEST SESSIONS · PARIS & AMSTERDAM

Custom projects developed between Brazil and Europe

Between August and September, Jéssica Santana will be tattooing in Paris and Amsterdam with limited availability for projects inspired by personal stories, references and experiences.

Jéssica Santana is a visual artist, tattoo artist and mentor with 9 years of experience creating custom projects that combine art, composition and visual identity.

Alongside her tattoo work, Jé has also mentored and trained more than a hundred tattoo artists, sharing her perspective on artistic construction, skin reading and developing projects with more intention and presence.

Her work moves between characters, tribute pieces, emotional projects, visual references and custom compositions, always respecting the story behind each choice and the way it naturally fits the body.
